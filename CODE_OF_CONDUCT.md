# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent, give specific and kind feedback, and keep discussions about the project.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information (including receipts), or repeated off-topic or promotional posts.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules and may block people who keep doing so. To report a problem, contact the maintainer via the email address on their GitHub profile.

This is a short summary in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
