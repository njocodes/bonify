## 0.1.0

First public version.

- Import REWE eBon PDFs (or a ZIP); everything is read locally, payment details on the receipt are never stored
- Overview: spending per month, trips, average basket, categories, stores, payment methods, VAT
- Products with price history, a "got more expensive" list that ignores one-off offers, and a personal inflation index
- Habits: weekday and hour heatmap, time between trips, checkout time, spending calendar
- Bonus balance, coupons, discounts and deposit
- Editable product categories, period filter, backup export and import
- German and English, Classic and Clean design, offline PWA
